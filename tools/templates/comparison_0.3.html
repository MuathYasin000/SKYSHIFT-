    THE INSTRUMENT BEHIND THE IMAGES

    A different kind of light.
    A careful way to look.

    SPHEREx surveys the infrared sky. Each detector image samples different wavelengths across its surface. Two pictures of the same field are not automatically a fair time comparison.

    SKYSHIFT keeps that context visible, preserves the original evidence, and helps another person reconstruct your view. This release supports a small, curated review field.
