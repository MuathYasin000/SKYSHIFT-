The five challenge targets

Each target opens cached 8′ cutouts from NASA/IPAC IRSA. More observations may be available from the live archive. The dates, detector, wavelength and source links are visible in the viewer.

Barnard’s Star
The marker uses the catalog position at each exposure date. The bundled preview target pixels carry archive flags and are masked.
Epsilon Indi B
Browse the observations of this nearby brown dwarf pair. The displayed sampling limits what you can resolve.
WISE 0855-0714
Check multiple dates and detector bands around this cold brown dwarf. Check the catalog position propagated to each exposure date.
RW Aurigae
Compare frames across dates and check the wavelength and mask details before interpreting image differences.
R Draconis
Inspect the mask around this pulsating red giant. The bundled preview target pixels are masked. Read the native flags in Details.
