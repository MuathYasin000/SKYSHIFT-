Native data diagnostics

Background is the median of valid pixels, including sources and diffuse emission. It is not a fitted sky level. Image variance describes spatial spread; the VARIANCE extension describes native measurement uncertainty.

Source & mask evidence

Missing coverage outside the cutout is distinct from a flagged or nonfinite sample inside it. Bright or dark usable pixels cannot be identified as detector defects from appearance alone.
