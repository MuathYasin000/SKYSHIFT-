Options & data notes

ICRS coordinates: decimal RA/Dec in degrees; hms RA in hours (0–24). Width is the side of a square, 1–30 arcmin. One to six cutouts per request; larger fields may take longer. Conservative quality masks exclude unsupported pixels in every mode.

Discovery indexes metadata across the four supported collections with up to eight bounded requests. TAP also samples authoritative calibration links within that limit. It downloads no observation FITS and does not approve temporal comparisons. Service ingestion times can differ.

Observations and Compare open real DR1 and QR3 exposures with dates, detectors, wavelength labels and quality masks. Use the image controls to look for differences and the source records to check them.

Live requests need this host’s preparation service; cached examples work without it. Coordinates and field width go to this host and the public archive. Your notes stay in your browser until exported.
