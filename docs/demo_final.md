## 30-second storyboard

| Time | Screen and action | On-screen line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Open Observations. Show RA, Dec, the 8′ field, and all five presets. Choose RW Aurigae. | Choose a place in the sky. |
| 5–10 s | Show the filmstrip thumbnails and frame details, including the wavelength and archive link. | Real NASA SPHEREx exposures. |
| 10–17 s | Press Play in the viewer and pause on a labelled frame. | Blink through single observations. |
| 17–24 s | In Compare, choose frames A and B and show them side by side, then move the wipe. Leave the dates and any warnings visible. | Check the detector and wavelength for both dates. |
| 24–30 s | Show the source, mask label, and Share view button. | NASA/IPAC IRSA · Display differences are exploratory. |

## Seven-slide outline

1. **SKYSHIFT** — the Observations entry screen and one sentence about exploring real infrared observations over time.
2. **Pick a place** — RA and Dec fields, 8′ setting, and the five target buttons.
3. **Inspect observations** — a crop of dated rows with detector, wavelength, release, and archive ID.
4. **Blink through dates** — two captures from the single-frame timeline with their dates visible.
5. **Compare two dates** — side by side and wipe using the suggested pair. Keep the release or wavelength warning if it appears.
6. **Read the image** — target marker, labelled masks, and the archive source. Include the display-only gap fill toggle.
7. **Sources and limits** — DR1 and QR3 credits, partial cache and live archive availability, no detection or motion claim, and the verified public link.

The public cache checks used the hosted site and all five challenge targets.
