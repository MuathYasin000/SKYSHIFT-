# SKYSHIFT submission demo

Use the browser on the [deployed Observations page](https://astronomy-licensed-made-successfully.trycloudflare.com/explore.html?to=12) and the Compare page it opens. Keep the interface, dates, masks, and wavelength warning in the frame.

## 30-second storyboard

| Time | Screen and action | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | Start on Observations. Point out the right ascension and declination fields, then click Barnard’s Star to fill them. | “Start with sky coordinates or pick one of the five challenge targets.” |
| 6–13 s | Scroll to the dated results. Show the first thumbnails and the detector and wavelength columns. | “SKYSHIFT lists real SPHEREx exposures, with the date, wavelength, and detector for each image.” |
| 13–21 s | Click **Open these frames in Compare**, then **Play**. Keep the frame strip and date label visible as they advance. | “Blink through the observations to see how the same field looks across dates.” |
| 21–30 s | Pause, choose two frames, and drag **Wipe**. Hold the wavelength warning on screen at the end. | “Wipe between any two exposures. Wavelength changes can alter brightness, so this is a starting point for investigation.” |

## Seven-slide outline

| Slide | Content | Deployed screen to use |
|---|---|---|
| 1. SKYSHIFT | Explore real SPHEREx observations across dates. | Compare viewer with a Barnard’s Star frame. |
| 2. Enter a sky position | Show right ascension, declination, field size, and the five presets. | Observations search form. |
| 3. Browse the observations | Follow the example from Barnard’s Star into its dated results. | Observation list with thumbnails and detector labels. |
| 4. Check coverage | Explain the bundled frames and live IRSA lookup. | Barnard’s Star cache count and **Load remaining from IRSA**. |
| 5. Blink | Show how Play and the frame strip move the viewer through time. | Compare in flipbook mode. |
| 6. Compare two frames | Show the wipe slider and the wavelength warning. | Compare in wipe mode. |
| 7. Try it and cite the data | Invite visitors to choose a target, then credit NASA/IPAC IRSA and SPHEREx DR1 and QR3. | Challenge target buttons or the Compare metadata panel. |
